FAQ

Dumpster rental in Los Angeles: FAQ

How much is a dumpster rental in Los Angeles?

A 16-yard roll-off dumpster in Los Angeles is $550 flat, including delivery, 7 days on site, pickup and up to 1.5 tons of disposal.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Los Angeles?

No permit is needed when the dumpster sits on your driveway or private property. If it needs to go on a public street, check with the City of Los Angeles first.

Which Los Angeles ZIP codes do you serve?

We deliver to all of Los Angeles, including ZIP codes 90004, 90005, 90006, 90010, 90019, 90020, 90026, 90027, 90029, 90036, 90039, 90048.

How big is a 16-yard dumpster?

A 16-yard dumpster holds 16 cubic yards of debris, which is roughly 6 to 8 pickup-truck loads. It's big enough for a garage or home cleanout or a kitchen or bathroom remodel, and small enough to fit in most residential driveways.

How much weight is included?

Up to 1.5 tons (3,000 lbs) of disposal is included. Light, bulky items like furniture and household junk rarely reach that limit. Heavy materials like concrete, dirt, brick, rock and tile add weight quickly, so fill levels for those will be lower.

What can't go in the dumpster?

No hazardous materials (chemicals, fuels, solvents, asbestos, pesticides), no tires and no paint. If you're not sure about an item, call us before you toss it.
